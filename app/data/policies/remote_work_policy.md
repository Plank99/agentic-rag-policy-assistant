# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
Employees whose role does not require physical presence on-site may work remotely up to
**3 days per week**, subject to manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require VP-level
sign-off and must be documented in the HR system.

## Work Hours and Availability
Remote employees must be reachable during the core collaboration window of **10:00–16:00**
local time and must keep their calendar and status in the chat tool up to date.

## Equipment
Employees working remotely are entitled to a one-time home-office stipend of up to **400 EUR**
for ergonomic equipment (chair, monitor, keyboard), reimbursed via the standard expense
process. Company laptops and peripherals remain company property and must be returned upon
termination.

## Security Requirements
Remote work requires a private, password-protected Wi-Fi network and the corporate VPN client
for any access to internal systems. Working from public Wi-Fi (airports, cafes) without a VPN
connection is prohibited, in line with the IT Security Policy.

## International Remote Work
Working remotely from a country other than the employee's registered country of employment for
more than 10 consecutive business days requires prior approval from HR and Legal, due to tax
and labor law implications.
