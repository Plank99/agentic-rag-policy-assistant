# Data Protection & GDPR Policy

## Scope
This policy applies to all processing of personal data of employees, customers, and
prospects, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## Data Minimization
Only personal data strictly necessary for a defined business purpose may be collected and
retained. Customer personal data must not be exported to personal devices or non-approved
cloud storage.

## Data Subject Requests
Requests from individuals to access, correct, or delete their personal data ("data subject
requests") must be forwarded to the Data Protection Officer (DPO) within **24 hours** of
receipt by filing a support ticket with category `legal_data_subject_request` and priority
`high`. GDPR requires a response within **30 calendar days**.

## Data Breach Notification
Any suspected personal data breach must be reported internally within **1 hour** of discovery
(see IT Security Policy incident reporting) so that the DPO can assess whether the 72-hour
regulatory notification deadline to the supervisory authority applies.

## Data Retention
Customer personal data is retained for no longer than **24 months** after the end of the
business relationship, unless a longer period is required by law (e.g., tax records).

## Third-Party Processors
Any new vendor that will process personal data on the company's behalf must be reviewed and
approved by Legal and must sign a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) before data is shared.
