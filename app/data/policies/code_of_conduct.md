# Code of Conduct

## Core Principles
All employees are expected to act with integrity, respect, and professionalism in every
interaction with colleagues, clients, and partners.

## Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any financial or personal relationship that could reasonably be seen
as a conflict of interest with company business, including outside employment, board
positions, or significant shareholdings in a competitor, client, or supplier.

## Anti-Harassment and Non-Discrimination
The company has zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination based on race, gender, age,
religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic. Concerns can
be reported confidentially via a support ticket with category `hr_conduct_concern`, which is
routed directly to HR leadership rather than the employee's direct manager.

## Gifts and Entertainment
Employees may accept business gifts with a value under **75 EUR**. Gifts above this threshold
must be declined or reported to the Compliance team.

## Confidentiality
Employees must not disclose confidential company, client, or employee information to any
third party, both during and after their employment, except as required by law.

## Reporting Violations
Suspected violations of this Code of Conduct should be reported via a support ticket with
category `hr_conduct_concern`. Retaliation against anyone reporting in good faith is strictly
prohibited.
