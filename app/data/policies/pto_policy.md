# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

## Accrual
Full-time employees accrue paid time off at a rate of **1.75 days per full calendar month** of
employment, equivalent to **21 days per year**. Accrual begins on the employee's official hire
date and is prorated for the first partial month of employment.

## Carryover
At the end of the calendar year, employees may carry over a maximum of **5 unused PTO days**
into the following year. Any unused balance above 5 days is forfeited on January 1st, unless
local labor law requires otherwise.

## Requesting PTO
PTO requests must be submitted at least 5 business days in advance through the HR portal.
Requests shorter than 5 business days require manager approval via a support ticket with
category `hr_leave_request`.

## Sick Leave
Sick leave is tracked separately from PTO and does not accrue; employees are entitled to
10 paid sick days per year, reset every January 1st.

## Unpaid Leave
Employees who have exhausted their PTO balance may request unpaid leave by filing a support
ticket with category `hr_leave_request`, subject to manager approval.
