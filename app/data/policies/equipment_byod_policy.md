# Equipment & BYOD (Bring Your Own Device) Policy

## Company-Issued Equipment
New employees receive a standard-issue laptop, charger, and headset within their first week.
Additional peripherals (second monitor, docking station) can be requested via a support
ticket with category `it_equipment_request`.

## BYOD for Mobile Access
Employees may use personal mobile devices to access corporate email and chat applications
only after enrolling the device in the company's Mobile Device Management (MDM) system, which
enforces a passcode and remote-wipe capability. Personal devices may never be used to access
systems containing customer data.

## Equipment Return
All company-issued equipment must be returned within **5 business days** of the employment
end date. Unreturned equipment may be deducted from the final paycheck where permitted by
local law.

## Loss or Damage
Lost or damaged equipment must be reported immediately via a support ticket with category
`it_equipment_request` and priority `high`, so that IT can remotely wipe the device if
necessary and arrange a replacement.

## Upgrade Cycle
Laptops are refreshed on a **3-year cycle**. Employees with performance-intensive roles
(e.g., engineering, data science) may request an earlier refresh with manager justification.
