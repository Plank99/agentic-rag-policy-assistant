# IT Security Policy

## Password Requirements
All corporate accounts must use a password of at least **12 characters**, including upper and
lower case letters, a digit, and a symbol. Passwords must be rotated every **180 days** and may
not be reused for the previous 5 rotations. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for
all systems that access customer data.

## VPN Access
Remote access to internal systems requires the corporate VPN client. VPN accounts are
provisioned automatically for new hires but must be manually requested for contractors by
filing a support ticket with category `it_access_request`.

## Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident (phishing email, lost device, unauthorized access) must be
reported to the Security Operations team within **1 hour** of discovery via a support ticket
with category `it_security_incident` and priority `high`.

## Device Encryption
All company-issued laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled (BitLocker on Windows,
FileVault on macOS) before being granted access to the internal network.

## Software Installation
Employees may not install unapproved third-party software on company devices. Requests for new
software must go through IT via a support ticket with category `it_access_request`.
