# Business Travel Policy

## Booking
All business travel (flights, trains, hotels) must be booked through the corporate travel
portal at least **7 days** in advance when possible, to secure negotiated rates. Bookings made
outside the portal require prior Finance approval.

## Flight Class
Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights
between 6 and 9 hours. Business class requires VP approval and is limited to flights over
9 hours.

## Lodging
Hotel bookings should not exceed the nightly caps defined in the Expense Reimbursement Policy
(200 EUR/night in major cities, 130 EUR/night elsewhere) without prior approval.

## International Travel Safety
Employees traveling internationally to a location flagged as elevated-risk by the corporate
travel-security provider must file a support ticket with category `travel_safety_approval`
at least 10 business days before departure.

## Travel Cancellations
If a trip is cancelled after booking, employees must notify Finance immediately via the
expense system so that refundable costs can be recovered; non-refundable costs related to a
business-justified cancellation are not charged back to the employee.
