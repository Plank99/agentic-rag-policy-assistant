# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## General Rules
Business expenses must be submitted within **30 days** of being incurred, through the expense
management system, with an itemized receipt attached for any amount over **25 EUR**. Expenses
submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed without VP approval.

## Travel Expenses
Reasonable costs for airfare (economy class for flights under 6 hours), ground transportation,
and lodging (up to 200 EUR/night in major cities, 130 EUR/night elsewhere) are reimbursable
when related to approved business travel. See the Travel Policy for booking requirements.

## Meals
Meal reimbursement while traveling is capped at **60 EUR/day**, covering breakfast, lunch, and
dinner combined. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at pre-approved client entertainment events.

## Client Entertainment
Client entertainment expenses require prior manager approval and must list all attendees and
the business purpose on the expense report.

## Disputed or Missing Receipts
If a receipt is lost, employees must file a support ticket with category
`finance_expense_dispute` and provide a signed statement describing the expense; amounts over
100 EUR without a receipt require Finance Director approval.
